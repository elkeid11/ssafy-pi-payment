SSAFY Privacy Policy

SSAFY is a Pi utility app for community learning, project sharing, and AI automation planning.

Information we use

The app may request Pi authentication permissions such as username and payments scope when opened inside Pi Browser. This is used for app access and Test-Pi payment validation.

Local data

Checklist items, project ideas, and planning notes may be stored locally on the user's device through browser storage.

Payments

Test-Pi payment data is processed only for validating app payment functionality.

Contact

For questions, contact the app owner through the Pi Developer account connected to SSAFY.
